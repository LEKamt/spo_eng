03:57–04:25 — SLIDE 11: SHANNON ENTROPY, ANNEX B
Annex B also quantifies speed variability. We use eight bins one metre per second wide, and thirty-one frames around each of three speed peaks. Shannon entropy is about 2.9 bits in each stroke window, near the three-bit maximum. The whole trial is lower at 2.443 bits because it includes many slow frames. This describes speed occupancy, not skill, temporal predictability or perceived affordance. Multiply bits by the natural logarithm of two to obtain nats.

04:25–04:55 — SLIDE 12: MUTUAL INFORMATION, ANNEX B
Across three stroke windows and four ball locations, C1 intersects in all three windows, C2 in one, and C3 and C4 in none. Near versus far reduces the empirical outcome uncertainty from 0.918 to 0.459 bits: a gain of 0.459 bits. These are twelve modeled combinations, not twelve independent sporting trials. Shannon quantifies uncertainty in this model; Gibson concerns information available for action. Observer perception remains to be measured.

LIKELY QUESTIONS
Why does C4 have a different timestamp? The criterion minimizes distance across all 379 frames. Its nearest approach is at f101, still outside the threshold.
Why not say the actor cannot reach C3? The player might adapt footwork, trunk or arm motion. This test freezes a recorded path.
Does high Shannon entropy mean better performance? No. It describes occupancy over selected speed bins and ignores temporal ordering.
Does KL measure different movement trajectories? Not here. It compares speed histograms. Similar histograms can come from different temporal or spatial trajectories.
Is C2's classification robust? It is deterministic for these data and assumptions, but the small margin requires a calibrated spatial-error analysis. Cross-pipeline hand-height discrepancy is not itself a proxy-error distribution.
Did we collect judgments? No. The protocol and ideal-observer example are explicitly proposed or illustrative.
What is still missing for full fidelity evidence? Matching OpenSim pose screenshots and investigation of the hand-height mismatch. Export plots cannot substitute for a claimed live OpenSim pose capture.
